\begin{abstract}
Estimating binned angular power spectra from masked high-resolution full-sky maps---through spherical harmonic transforms and pseudo-$C_\ell$ (MASTER) estimators that propagate survey windows into mode coupling---remains a practical bottleneck in CMB and large-scale structure pipelines. On GPUs the latitudinal factor of a HEALPix transform is often evaluated as a serial Jacobi recurrence in degree; in float32 that recurrence is unstable near the poles and at turning points, where a rounding of size $u\lvert v\rvert$ is amplified by $1/\sin\phi$. We rewrite the march so that the carried pair is $(v_n,D_n)$ with $D_n=v_n-v_{n-1}$ rather than $(v_n,v_{n-1})$. The difference coefficient vanishes as $\phi^2$ exactly where the plain form is ill-conditioned, error is injected at the scale of $D\sim\phi\,v$, and the float32 floor becomes the random walk $\sqrt{n}\,u$. Against the float64 recurrence at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=1024$ the maximum relative error over every lane falls from $4.2\times10^{-2}$ (plain) to $2.8\times10^{-6}$ (shipped). The identities of the rewrite, a southern-hemisphere reflection onto the same code path, and an exact per-block power-of-two rescale are proved in Lean~4. The kernel is CUDA, called from JAX through the XLA FFI, and is the default latitudinal operator in GMaster, a GPU library with the public API of NaMaster. We benchmark GMaster against widely used state-of-the-art methods and find $20\times$ improvement in end-to-end spin-$0$ MASTER estimation at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=1024$, $22\times$ at $2048$, and $12\times$ at $8192$, relative to NaMaster~3.0 on 96 CPU cores, with bandpowers agreeing to better than $4\times10^{-4}$ at all multipoles on the ACT DR6 scaling board ($N_{\mathrm{side}}=256$--$8192$), at least $17\times$ below the cosmic-variance limit of the bandpowers; the accuracy cost is sub-dominant to survey cosmic variance throughout.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The MASTER estimator \citep{hivon2002} recovers binned angular power spectra from masked spherical maps by analysing the masked fields, forming coupled pseudo-spectra, building a mode-coupling matrix from the mask, and decoupling. Throughout, $\ell$ and $m$ denote the usual spherical-harmonic multipole and azimuthal order, $\theta\in[0,\pi]$ and $\phi\in[0,2\pi)$ are colatitude and azimuth on the sphere, $s$ is the spin of the field, $N_{\mathrm{side}}$ is the HEALPix resolution parameter, and $\ell_{\max}=3N_{\mathrm{side}}-1$ unless stated otherwise.

NaMaster \citep{alonso2019} is the standard CPU implementation of that pipeline: \texttt{pymaster}~3.0 exposes \texttt{NmtField}, \texttt{NmtWorkspace}, and decoupled bandpowers with the normalisations used in current CMB and LSS analyses. Its HEALPix transforms call DUCC0 \citep{reinecke2013}, the successor of libsharp, on the HEALPix pixelisation \citep{gorski2005}. The coupling algebra has a structured Wigner-$3j$ recurrence \citep{kiddier2026}. We time NaMaster~3.0 with \texttt{ducc0}~0.39.1 on the same host as GMaster.

SHTns \citep{schaeffer2013} is a widely used pseudospectral library for on-the-fly associated-Legendre recurrences; release~3.7.5 adds a CUDA implementation (\url{https://nschaeff.bitbucket.io/shtns/}). Our transform timings use that GPU module (\texttt{SHT\_ALLOW\_GPU}) from a current source build wired in \texttt{benchmarks/scaling/bench\_three\_way.py}, not the 2013 journal release in isolation. SHTns has no spin-$2$ transform; spin-$2$ panels time its spin-$1$ vector transform as a GPU reference. We do not quote SHTns float32 transform timings: its on-the-fly recurrence is unstable in float32 for the same $1/\sin\phi$ reason as the plain Jacobi march below. GMaster is not benchmarked as a drop-in replacement for arbitrary SHTns grids; the comparison is isolated \texttt{alm2map}/\texttt{map2alm} passes at matched $\ell_{\max}$.

On a GPU the latitudinal stage remains a serial chain in $\ell$ with a colatitude reduction at every degree---a good match to AVX-512 CPUs, not to SIMT. Precomputed Legendre tables \citep{price2024} invert the bottleneck when they fit; other GPU spherical-harmonic work \citep{belkner2024,reinecke2023} is not a drop-in HEALPix MASTER pipeline.

GMaster is a JAX implementation of the NaMaster public API. This Letter isolates one change that made a table-free float32 latitudinal kernel accurate enough to be the default at every $N_{\mathrm{side}}\ge64$: marching the difference pair of the Jacobi row attached to $d^\ell_{m,-s}(\theta)$ rather than the consecutive values. The recurrence, a reflection that sends the southern hemisphere through the same instructions, and the exactness of a per-block power-of-two rescale are proved in Lean~4 \citep{demoura2021,mathlib2020}. We report the end-to-end MASTER board and a real-map overlay on ACT DR6. This is not a cosmological analysis: no beam, split cross-spectrum, or covariance is applied to the survey maps.

\section{Difference-form march}

Every Wigner function $d^\ell_{m,-s}(\theta)$ is an algebraic multiple of a Jacobi polynomial \citep[NIST DLMF 14.9.16, 18.3]{olver2010},
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
  d^\ell_{m,-s}(\theta)
  &= (-1)^m\,2^{\varepsilon_m\mathrm{LG2N}}\\
  &\quad\times
    \Bigl(\sin\tfrac{\theta}{2}\Bigr)^\alpha
    \Bigl(\cos\tfrac{\theta}{2}\Bigr)^\beta
    P_n^{(\alpha,\beta)}(\cos\theta),
\end{split}
\end{equation}
with $\alpha=m+s$, $\beta=\lvert m-s\rvert$, $n=\ell-\max(m,s)$ and $\mathrm{LG2N}=\log_2\sqrt{(\ell+m)!(\ell-m)!/((\ell-s)!(\ell+s)!)}$. The family obeys a three-term recurrence in $n$ \citep[DLMF 18.9.1]{olver2010}. Writing $x=\cos\theta$ and $v_n$ for the marched polynomial,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:jacobi}
  v_n = A_n v_{n-1}-B_n v_{n-2},\qquad A_n=c_1 x+c_0,\quad B_n=c_2,
\end{equation}
where $c_1,c_0,c_2$ depend only on $(\ell,m)$. A float64 march of~\eqref{eq:jacobi} agrees with a numpy oracle of the same coefficients to $1.5\times10^{-15}$ at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=1024$. Plain float32 of the same recurrence reaches a maximum relative error of $4.2\times10^{-2}$ over the row (Figure~\ref{fig:accuracy}, left).

Over a few degrees the coefficients are nearly constant and $c_2\to1$, so the row is locally $v_n\approx A v_{n-1}-v_{n-2}$ with roots $e^{\pm i\phi}$ and $2\sqrt{c_2}\cos\phi=A$. In the oscillatory region $\lvert A\rvert<2$, and $\phi\to0$ at the poles and at turning points $m\approx(\ell+\tfrac12)\sin\theta$. A rounding $\delta\sim 2u\lvert v\rvert$ of $\mathrm{fl}(Av_{k-1}-Bv_{k-2})$ is amplified by $1/\sin\phi$ on each mode, giving a relative random-walk floor $\sim\sqrt{n}\,u/\sin\phi$; on the first HEALPix ring at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=4096$ this is a few per cent (Figure~\ref{fig:accuracy}, left). A single-word $x$ is worse: it drives a coherent phase drift $\sim n\,c_1x\,\epsilon/(2\sin\phi)$.

Let $D_n=v_n-v_{n-1}$. Then for $n\ge2$
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:diff}
  D_n=(A_n-1-B_n)\,v_{n-1}+B_n D_{n-1},\qquad v_n=v_{n-1}+D_n.
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:diff} is the central result of this Letter: the latitudinal march is carried in difference form, and every subsequent kernel design choice (two-word $C$, reflection, rescale) is in service of making that recurrence stable in float32.
In terms of $\phi$, the difference coefficient is $C=A-1-c_2\approx-\phi^2$, so $D\approx\phi\,\lvert v\rvert$ exactly where the plain march is ill-conditioned. A rounding of $v\leftarrow\mathrm{fl}(v+D)$ lands on $v$ with $D$ fixed and is not amplified. A rounding of $D\leftarrow\mathrm{fl}(c_2 D+C v)$ lands on $D$ at scale $u\phi\lvert v\rvert$ and, after the $1/(2\sin\phi)$ modal factor, contributes $O(u\lvert v\rvert)$ per step. Both kinds therefore give a random-walk floor $\sqrt{n}\,u$ independent of $\phi$: $\sqrt{3072}\,u\approx3.3\times10^{-6}$ at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=1024$, against the measured $2.8\times10^{-6}$.

The only coefficient on the amplified path is $C$, and the argument needs its error to be $O(u\lvert C\rvert)$, not $O(u\lvert A\rvert)$. Writing $C_n=c_1(x-1)+E_n$ with $E_n=c_1-1-c_2+c_0$, the kernel carries $x-1$ rather than $x$: near a pole $x-1\sim-\theta^2/2$, so a single-word $x$ would inject a relative error $u/(\theta^2/2)$ into the leading term of $C$, and a single-word $E$ would leave an error $\sim u\lvert c_1\rvert$ that does not shrink with $C$. Both are systematic and drift the phase. The kernel therefore stores $\lvert x\rvert-1=x_h+x_l$, $c_1=c_{1h}+c_{1l}$ and $E=E_h+E_l$ as float32 pairs, each split from float64 to $2^{-48}$ \citep{dekker1971}, and forms
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:Cfma}
  C=\mathrm{fma}(c_{1h},x_h,E_h)+\bigl[\mathrm{fma}(c_{1h},x_l,\mathrm{fma}(c_{1l},x_h,E_l))\bigr].
\end{equation}
The first \texttt{fma} rounds once, by $u\lvert C\rvert$, and that rounding vanishes with $C$ at the turning point where the amplification peaks; the bracket is $\sim2^{-24}$ of the leading terms. A single-word $C$ leaves the coherent $u\lvert c_1\rvert$-class drift, growing like $n$ rather than $\sqrt n$. The coefficient $c_2$ is single-word, because its error enters as $u\lvert D\rvert\sim u\phi\lvert v\rvert$ on $D$, the unamplified kind.

The southern hemisphere needs no second code path. If $v_n=(c_1x+c_0)v_{n-1}-c_2v_{n-2}$ then $w_n:=(-1)^nv_n$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:refl}
  w_n=\bigl(c_1(-x)-c_0\bigr)w_{n-1}-c_2w_{n-2},
\end{equation}
the same recurrence with $y=\lvert x\rvert$ for $x$ and $-c_0$ for $c_0$, whose difference coefficient is $c_1(y-1)+E^-_n$ with $E^-_n=c_1-1-c_2-c_0$. Every lane, northern or southern, therefore marches $\lvert x\rvert-1\in(-1,0]$ with the same instructions and one of two table columns $E^\pm$, and the emit restores the sign on odd $n$. Each lane stores $v\cdot2^{ex}$ with $ex$ an integer and is rescaled by an exact power of two every eight degrees; the map $(v,D)\mapsto(v+D',D')$ is linear, so the represented value is unchanged, and the per-degree growth is bounded by $1+\lvert C\rvert+c_2\le2^{15}$, so eight degrees cannot overflow. The emit factorises the Jacobi normalisation $2^{\varepsilon_m\mathrm{LG2N}}$ into a per-lane power of two and a per-degree mantissa proved to lie in $[2^{-45.5},2)$, so no exponent lane leaves the kernel. Equations~\eqref{eq:diff} and~\eqref{eq:refl}, the seeds, and the rescale are theorems in a Lean~4 development on Mathlib with no \texttt{sorry}; the modal error argument is checked by Figure~\ref{fig:accuracy} (left).

The kernel is CUDA, one warp per $(m$, ring tile), exposed as XLA FFI handlers inside \texttt{jit}; field and mask share one analysis launch. The default at $L=\ell_{\max}+1\ge192$ ($N_{\mathrm{side}}\ge64$) is this march; float64 remains available by flag.

\section{Validation and performance}

All timings are single-tenant on one NVIDIA RTX~PRO~6000 Blackwell (96~GB); NaMaster~3.0 / \texttt{ducc0}~0.39.1 on a 96-core Threadripper~PRO~9995WX is the CPU reference (Figure~\ref{fig:scoreboard}). End-to-end totals are warmed medians of field construction, coupling matrix, coupled cell and decoupling. Decoupled bandpower agreement $|C_\ell^{\mathrm{GM}}/C_\ell^{\mathrm{NM}}-1|$ on the ACT DR6 scaling board uses native-resolution maps at each $N_{\mathrm{side}}$ (linear bins of $30$). After switching mode-coupling Gauss--Legendre weights to \texttt{ducc0}, bandpowers agree with NaMaster to better than $4\times10^{-4}$ at all multipoles from $N_{\mathrm{side}}=256$ to $8192$, at least $17\times$ below cosmic variance; at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=8192$ TT maxima fall from $7.1\times10^{-3}$ to $3.8\times10^{-5}$ and EE from $7.3\times10^{-3}$ to $2.8\times10^{-4}$ (Figure~\ref{fig:accuracy}). On the ACT coadd at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=4096$ (Figure~\ref{fig:act}), decoupled TT has $\mathrm{rms}(C_\ell^{\mathrm{GM}}/C_\ell^{\mathrm{NM}}-1)=1.41\times10^{-5}$ in $7.63\,\mathrm{s}$ versus $70.4\,\mathrm{s}$ on $192$ cores.

\section{Conclusions}

A three-term Jacobi recurrence in float32 is limited by $1/\sin\phi$ amplification, not by the width of its words. Marching $(v,v-v_{\mathrm{prev}})$ moves the injection of every rounding onto a difference that already vanishes at the turning points, and a two-word $C$ removes the residual coherent drift. Carried as a CUDA kernel behind NaMaster's API, that rewrite is the default latitudinal operator in GMaster and is $10$--$22\times$ NaMaster at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=128$--$8192$ on the board of Figure~\ref{fig:scoreboard}. The same pipeline on an ACT DR6 coadd at $N_{\mathrm{side}}=4096$ agrees at $10^{-5}$ in under nine seconds.

No other GPU spherical-harmonic implementation was timed on the full MASTER board. The shipped default trades NaMaster's float64 transform contract for decoupled bandpowers that match NaMaster to better than $4\times10^{-4}$ at all multipoles on the ACT scaling board ($N_{\mathrm{side}}=256$--$8192$), at least $17\times$ below cosmic variance; the exact float64 route is one flag. The overlay is a methods comparison, not a science product.
